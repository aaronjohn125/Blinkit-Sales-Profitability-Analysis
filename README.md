# 🛒 Blinkit Sales & Profitability Analysis

A Python-based business analytics project analysing **121,000+ records across multiple datasets** to understand revenue, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, and delivery efficiency.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyse overall revenue and order performance
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Analyse category-wise profitability
- Measure customer retention and repeat purchases
- Identify high-value customers
- Evaluate delivery performance and delay reasons
- Analyse payment methods and revenue contribution
- Understand revenue concentration across products

## 🛠️ Tools

**Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Jupyter Notebook**

## 🔍 Key Findings

- 💰 **₹4.97M** total revenue
- 🧾 **121,000+ records** analysed
- 🔄 **94.2% repeat-customer rate**
- 🚚 **69.4% on-time delivery rate**
- 📦 Revenue and profitability analysed across multiple product categories
- 🏆 Identified the top revenue-generating products and highest-value customers

## 💡 Business Insight

The project combines multiple datasets to move from raw transactional data to business insights around **customer retention, product concentration, profitability, and operational efficiency**.

It highlights how data can be used to identify both **growth opportunities and operational drawbacks** in a quick-commerce business.
